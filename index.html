CampusSwap

CampusSwap is a peer-to-peer online marketplace designed exclusively for university students to buy, sell, and trade secondhand college essentials. From textbooks, laptops, and scientific calculators to dorm furniture, lab gear, and stationery, CampusSwap connects students within the same campus community for fast, affordable, and safe local exchanges. By keeping transaction costs low and facilitating convenient on-campus handoffs, CampusSwap helps students save money, clear out unused gear, and reduce waste across campus.

Affordable College Gear For Students, by Students

College semesters are expensive enough without paying full retail price for laptops, scientific calculators, engineering textbooks, and dorm tech. CampusSwap gives pre-loved campus essentials a second life, connecting verified students for quick, budget-friendly exchanges right on campus.
